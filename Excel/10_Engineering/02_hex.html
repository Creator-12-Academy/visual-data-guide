💻 Formula Structure

  • Number: The hexadecimal string (e.g. "FF").
  • Limit: Up to 10 characters (40 bits).

⚠️ Notes

🎨 Color Codes
Web colors (e.g., #FF5733) use 3 pairs of Hex. Red=FF (255), Green=57 (87), Blue=33 (51). HEX2DEC helps break these down.
