💻 Formula Structure

=NETWORKDAYS(start, end, [holidays])
  • Logic: Counts days between Start & End.
  • Excludes: Saturdays, Sundays, and dates in [holidays].
=NETWORKDAYS.INTL(start, end, weekend, [hol])
  • Weekend Code: 1 = Sat/Sun, 7 = Fri/Sat, 11 = Sun only.
  • String Code: "0000011" (0=Work, 1=Off, Mon-Sun).

⚠️ Common Pitfalls

🚫 Inclusive Counting
Unlike simple subtraction (End - Start), this function is inclusive. Nov 1 to Nov 1 is 1 working day, not 0.
