⚠️ Common Pitfalls

🚫 Mon vs Sun Start
By default, Excel thinks weeks start on Sunday (Return Type 1).
If your work week is Mon-Sun, use WEEKDAY(A1, 2). This makes Mon=1, Sun=7.
📅 ISO Week Numbers
For European standards (ISO 8601), use ISOWEEKNUM instead of WEEKNUM. It handles the "Week 1" definition differently near Jan 1st.
