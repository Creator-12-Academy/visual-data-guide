💻 Formula Structure

=A1
  • Behavior: "Look 1 cell up/left relative to me."
  • Result: If moved to B2, it looks at B1.
=$A$1
  • Behavior: "Look exactly at cell A1, always."
  • Symbol: The $ acts as an anchor for the Column and/or Row.
