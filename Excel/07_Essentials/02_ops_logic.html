⚠️ Common Pitfalls

🚫 Text vs Number
"10" = 10 is FALSE. Text and Numbers are different types.
However, "Apple" = "apple" is TRUE (Excel is case-insensitive).
🔀 Not Equal Symbol
The symbol for "Not Equal" is <>. Think of it as "Less than OR Greater than".
