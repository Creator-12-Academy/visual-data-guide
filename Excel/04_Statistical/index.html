📊 Statistical Functions

Analyze your data. Learn to count, average, rank, and understand the distribution of your numbers.

📉 Essential
01. AVERAGE / AVERAGEA
The classic Mean. Learn the difference between averaging numbers and averaging text/booleans.
🎯 Crucial
02. AVERAGEIF / S
Find the average of specific items. E.g., "Average score for Class A only".
🔢 Basic
03. COUNT / COUNTA
Count cells with numbers (COUNT) vs cells that are not empty (COUNTA).
⬜ Utility
04. COUNTBLANK
Identify gaps in your data. Counts empty cells within a range.
🔎 Essential
05. COUNTIF / S
Count how many times a value appears. Great for frequency checks and duplicates.
📏 Basic
06. MIN / MAX
Find the extremes. Get the smallest (minimum) and largest (maximum) number in a set.
🏅 Ranking
07. LARGE / SMALL
Find the Nth value. "Who is the 2nd highest?" or "3rd lowest?".
🎯 Distribution
08. MEDIAN / MODE
Find the middle value (Median) or the most frequent value (Mode).
〰️ Advanced
09. STDEV.P / .S
Measure volatility. How much do values deviate from the average? (Population vs Sample).
🏆 Ranking
10. RANK.EQ / .AVG
Determine the standing of a number. Is it #1, #5, or #100 in the list?
